About the school

Educating the students of Gahkuch and Ghizer

The Learning Minds Higher Secondary School is located in Gahkuch, the main town of Ghizer district in Gilgit-Baltistan, Pakistan. The school provides higher secondary education to a student body of more than 400, guided by Principal Sir Hader and a team of subject teachers.
